Aura Farmer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how the Aura Farmer game ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, scores, settings and timing calibration locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

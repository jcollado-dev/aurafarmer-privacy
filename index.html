Aura Farmer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the Aura Farmer game ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app shows ads between runs through Google AdMob, sells a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play and RevenueCat, and has an optional online leaderboard. If you join the leaderboard, your chosen username and your best run's score are stored on our server and shown publicly.

Information we collect

Only if you choose to join the online leaderboard:

  • A username you pick. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name or anything that identifies you.
  • Your best run's results: score, accuracy, hit counts, longest combo, songs completed, farthest area reached, run length and the game version.
  • A random player key the app creates so that only your device can update your entry. We store only a one-way hash of it.

The app does not ask for an email, account or real name, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. If you never join the leaderboard, the app sends nothing to our server except requests to view the public leaderboard.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a full-screen ad between runs, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the app stops loading ads and the ad service entirely.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase provider, receives the purchase receipt and a random app user ID so it can confirm and restore your purchase. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is used only to run the leaderboard. Like any web server, the provider may briefly log technical request data such as IP addresses. We don't use those logs to identify you.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, scores, settings, timing calibration and leaderboard player key locally on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

Deleting your leaderboard entry

To have your username and score removed, email us the username and we will delete it.

Permissions

The app requests internet access (for ads, purchases and the leaderboard), network state (to check for a connection), the advertising ID (for ads) and Google Play billing (for the "Remove Ads" purchase).

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat and Supabase) for the purposes described. Your username and best run are public on the leaderboard by design.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
